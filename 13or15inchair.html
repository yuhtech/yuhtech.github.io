choosing between a 13" or a 15" macbook air?

here is everything you should know. for this case the budget is $2000.

Are you choosing between the 13" or the 15" macbook air? and what can you get for a $2000 budget? let's choose a m5 air!

if you choose the m5 macbook air 13" you get:

  • processing power: 10-core cpu, 10-core gpu, 16-core neural engine.
  • ram: for $2000 you can get 32gb of ram.
  • ssd storage: you get the higher-end 1tb disk for $2000.
  • power adapter: 40w dynamic power adapter with 60w max.
  • link to the build: buy the laptop at apple.
  • conclusion: this is the most powerful model for the money with a trade-off which is the screen estate.

and if you choose the m5 macbook air 15" you get:

  • processing power: 10-core cpu, 10-core gpu, 16-core neural engine.
  • ram: you can get the total of 24gb of ram to stay in-budget.
  • ssd storage: with the ram tradeoff you can still have 1tb ssd.
  • power adapter: 40w dynamic power adapter with 60w max.
  • link to the build: buy the laptop at apple.
  • conclusion: if you really care about the screen size, you still get a powerful laptop for the price.

what should YOU do?

if you don't really care about the screen size - get the 13", since the specs are better ALTHOUGH because it's smaller it will get hot more often. if you care about the screen size and just want a minimal trade-off you can get a 15" model.

"yes, the air is enough for coding and music-producing, although for video editing and heavy rendering a macbook pro wiuld be the best choice."

the prices may change, and you should decide based of your needs.
